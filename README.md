## Selected Projects

### [Leveraging-TDC-to-Bound-FDR](https://github.com/claisyd/Leveraging-TDC-to-Bound-FDR)
Research project for **SCDL3992** on bounding the False Discovery Rate under dependent p-values using a **Target–Decoy Competition** framework.

- Implemented the **FDR Bound** procedure in **R**
- Built simulation studies, evaluation metrics, and dependent p-value generators
- Developed plotting and analysis utilities for methodological comparison
- Focused on the intersection of **statistical theory**, **simulation**, and **computational methodology**

**Tools:** `R` `Probability Theory` `Target-Decoy Competition`

---

### [Probabilistic-Outlier-Detection-Circular-Data](https://github.com/claisyd/Probabilistic-Outlier-Detection-Circular-Data)
Research project for **SCDL3991** on probabilistic outlier detection in circular data.

- Implemented statistical methods in **R**
- Worked with the **EM algorithm**, confidence sets, and hypothesis testing
- Applied methods to real-world circular datasets
- Included analysis scripts and final report

**Tools:** `R (circular package)` `Circular Statistics` `EM Algorithm`

---

### [Numerical-Differentiation-Noisy-Data](https://github.com/claisyd/Numerical-Differentiation-Noisy-Data)
Independent computational study of numerical differentiation from data corrupted by additive Gaussian noise.

- Compared **forward, backward, and central differences** with fitting-based methods
- Investigated **polynomial least-squares fitting** and **cubic smoothing splines**
- Analysed finite-difference noise amplification and the smoothing trade-off
- Developed a noise-adaptive spline heuristic based on $s \approx n\sigma^2$
- Included reproducible Jupyter notebooks and a full research manuscript

**Tools:** `Python` `NumPy` `SciPy` `Numerical Analysis` `Smoothing Splines`

---

### [Supply-Chain-Management-Datathon-2025](https://github.com/claisyd/Supply-Chain-Management-Datathon-2025)
**1st place** submission for the **SUDATA x SUBAA Datathon 2025**.

- Developed **ETS time series forecasting** and **forecast reconciliation** workflows in **R**
- Implemented an **optimisation algorithm** for the supply chain management task
- Combined forecasting and optimisation to support decision-making in a competition setting
- Includes code and data from the team submission

**Tools:** `R` `Time Series` `Forecast Reconciliation` `Optimisation`

### [Stock-Prediction-Datathon-2024](https://github.com/claisyd/Stock-Prediction-Datathon-2024)
Project for the **SUDATA x SUBAA 2024 Datathon** on stock movement prediction.
